# Warning signs and failure prevention
Source: U.S. Fire Administration, Lithium-Ion Battery Safety for Electronic Devices
https://www.usfa.fema.gov/downloads/pdf/publications/lithium-ion-battery-safety-handout.pdf
Additional source: https://www.usfa.fema.gov/blog/lithium-ion-batteries/
Reviewed: 2026-10-23.

Use the manufacturer's recommended charger. Charge away from things that burn. Do not charge under a pillow or on bedding. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and use appropriately certified products. Battery pack repairs belong with certified repair facilities.

Stop using a lithium-ion battery if it swells, leaks, becomes unusually hot, changes color, makes odd noises, smells unusual or shows damage. Do not puncture, crush, open or attempt to repair a damaged pack. A battery that still powers a device can still be unsafe.

If there is smoke, fire, hissing or an immediate danger, move away, evacuate and contact local emergency services from a safe place. Do not handle or carry a smoking battery. For a swollen battery without an active fire, stop charging and using the device and seek professional service and local disposal guidance. Do not put lithium-ion batteries in household trash.
