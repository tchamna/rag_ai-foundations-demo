# Lead-acid care and chemistry differences
Source: Trojan Battery, Battery Maintenance
https://www.trojanbattery.com/resources/battery-maintenance
Sealed battery reference: https://www.trojanbattery.com/trojan-aes-batteries/aes-agm-standard-agm-battery-maintenance
Reviewed: 2026-10-23. Manufacturer guidance for Trojan products; use the exact battery's manual for settings and intervals.

Flooded lead-acid, sealed AGM and lithium-ion batteries have different maintenance and charger requirements. Never assume one charging voltage, maintenance routine or storage recommendation fits all types.

Battery service life depends on operating temperature, discharge depth, load, sizing and charging method. Use a compatible charger and the manufacturer's charging profile. Keep terminals clean and connections correctly secured according to the manual. Arrange professional inspection if charging or starting performance deteriorates.

Flooded lead-acid batteries contain corrosive electrolyte. Maintenance requires appropriate protective equipment and safe ventilation. Watering is specific to serviceable flooded batteries and must follow the manufacturer's procedure. Never add water, acid or additives to sealed AGM batteries. Equalization is not routine advice for all batteries; use only the manufacturer's specified procedure for an applicable battery. Do not open a sealed battery or improvise repairs.
