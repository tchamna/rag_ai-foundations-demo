# Lithium-ion health and everyday care
Source: Apple, Maximizing Battery Life and Lifespan
https://www.apple.com/batteries/maximizing-performance/
Reviewed: 2026-10-23. These recommendations describe Apple consumer devices; consult the manufacturer for other products.

Battery life is the time a device runs between charges. Battery lifespan is the period before its battery needs replacement. Heat can permanently reduce capacity. Avoid hot environments, direct sun and leaving devices in hot cars. Cold may temporarily shorten runtime; performance can recover after returning to normal temperature.

Use the device's optimized charging features when available. If charging causes excessive warmth with a case fitted, remove the case if the manufacturer recommends it. Reduce screen brightness and review energy-intensive background activity to extend runtime between charges.

For long-term storage of Apple devices, power down with approximately half charge and store in a cool, dry place. Check periodically and recharge during extended storage. Do not generalize one storage percentage or charging limit to every battery chemistry or vehicle; follow its manual.
